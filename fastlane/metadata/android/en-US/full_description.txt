Focus Study Gate is built on one idea: finish your work first, then spend what you earned.

You split your day into subjects with task lists and timers. Finishing tasks fills a daily treat bank of minutes. You spend those minutes at the gate in front of distracting apps, on Shorts and Reels, or in the built-in player.

<b>Study and rewards</b>
• Subjects with checklists, pausable timers, alarms and spoken reminders
• Sub tasks, weekly tasks and tasks planned for any date
• Streaks, XP, 30 badges and 17 short games for breaks
• Four home-screen widgets

<b>Phone control</b>
• A soft gate in front of the apps you choose: "What for?" with a short countdown and a choice of passes
• Not-free blocks: scheduled time windows where only an allow-list of apps works
• Counts YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok swipes correctly (it counts videos, not scroll events)
• Optional strict mode: instant gate, no "just this one", and a sentence you must type by hand to keep watching
• Covers apps in floating and split-screen windows
• Leave closes the app cleanly, and keeps browser tabs
• A usage screen like Digital Wellbeing

<b>Notes, reading and music</b>
• Notes with checklists, rich text, labels, voice notes and attachments
• Built-in PDF library and reader
• A music player with queue, playlists, equalizer and a media notification that phone-maker capsules pick up

<b>Private by design</b>
• No account, no server, no internet permission, no ads, no trackers
• English, Arabic (full right-to-left), Russian, German and French, plus importable language packs
• Free software under the AGPL-3.0

Focus Study Gate needs the usage access, display over other apps and accessibility permissions to work. The accessibility service is limited to YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and the browsers you gate, and only counts and times are stored.
