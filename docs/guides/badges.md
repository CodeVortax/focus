# Badges and XP

Everything here is derived from the per-day numbers Focus already keeps (`data/Game.kt`). Nothing is uploaded.

## Streaks

| Streak | Counts a day when |
|---|---|
| Work streak | You cleared the whole plate, or cashed the half-episode |
| Light-Shorts streak | You swiped 50 Shorts or fewer (a day you have already blown the limit breaks today's streak) |

## XP and levels

- Every 6 seconds of treat time earned gives 1 XP.
- For each past day, a Shorts bonus: 0 swipes = 250 XP, up to 10 = 150, up to 20 = 100, up to 50 = 50.
- Level = floor(sqrt(XP / 60)) + 1, so level N needs 60 x (N-1)^2 XP.

Level titles, in order: Couch Potato, Distracted Intern, Focus Apprentice, Task Goblin, Plate Wiper, Shorts Survivor, Deep Work Wizard, Thumb Monk, Study Samurai, Final Boss of Focus.

## Hall of Shame (10, hidden until earned)

These are honest, not punishing: they never remove XP or treats.

| | Badge | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| 🐐 | **Goat Neglecter** | 3 days in a row with tasks on the plate and none done. Come back before it's too late. |
| 🧟 | **Shorts Zombie** | 300 Shorts or more in a single day. |
| 🌙 | **Midnight Scroller** | Shorts between 1 and 5 in the morning. |
| 🏁 | **Shorts Marathon** | One single Shorts visit of an hour or more. |
| 👑 | **Overtime King** | Let a timer run 30 minutes over its budget. |
| 🚨 | **Emergency Abuser** | Use the locked-block emergency button 5 times. |
| 💬 | **Honesty Award** | Press "I'm distracted" 20 times. |
