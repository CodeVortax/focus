# Projects and the piggy bank

## Projects
For work that is not a checklist, like "my ESP IR project", add a **project** (home menu → Projects): a name and the apps you will use for it, for example Chrome, Termux and YouTube.

- **Start a session.** The apps of the project skip the gate and the **treat bank counts down, second by second, but only while one of those apps is on screen.** Thinking time away from the phone is free.
- **Warnings.** At 5 minutes and at 3 minutes of treat left, Focus sends a notification so you know you are nearly out.
- **The end.** When the bank is empty the session stops and the normal gate is back.
- Each project keeps the total time you spent on it. Shorts and Reels are still counted and gated inside a session.

## Piggy bank
Home menu → Piggy bank. The treat bank still resets every day; the piggy is what remembers.

- **Saving.** Every night, the treat time you did not spend goes into the piggy.
- **Payout.** Seven days later that amount is paid into your treat bank with **10% interest**. Payouts are spent first, so interest is never saved again.
- **Fines.** If you planned tasks for a day and finished none, you get a funny fine (speeding ticket, parking ticket, goat tax...) of 5 to 10 minutes, taken from the piggy, oldest savings first. With an empty piggy you only get a warning.
- Savings start counting from the day you update. A switch in the Piggy bank screen turns the whole thing off.
- A notification tells you about each payout and fine.
